Privacy Policy

Evaluation Profile Manager

Last updated: November 2026

Data We Collect

This app stores your evaluation data (soldier names, ranks, evaluation details, and profile numbers) locally on your device. If you enable iCloud, this data may be synced to your personal iCloud account.

How We Use Data

Data is used only to provide the features of the app. We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party.

Data Storage

All data remains on your device unless you choose to use iCloud sync or export a backup. We do not operate servers that receive your data.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer through the App Store listing.
